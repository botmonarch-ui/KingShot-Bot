Kingshot Bot for PC

Phantom is a free Kingshot bot for Windows. It plays your daily chores in BlueStacks: quests, rewards, Conquest, Intel missions and hero upgrades, on as many accounts as you run.

What the Kingshot auto farm bot does

Daily rewards

Quest rewards, VIP chest, sign-in gift, Path of Growth, mail, free hero recruits.

Combat

Conquest stages and idle chest, Intel missions (beast hunts and rescues).

Growth

Hero upgrades, chapter quest progression, Town Center upgrade, governor orders.

Safe by default

Nothing that spends gems, keys or speed-ups runs until you switch it on, always with a limit.

How to set it up

  1. Install BlueStacks 5 and Kingshot from the Play Store inside it.
  2. In BlueStacks, turn on Settings → Advanced → Android Debug Bridge.
  3. Download and run the Phantom installer (no administrator rights needed).
  4. Add your account, click Connect, choose features, then Start.

Questions

Is the Kingshot bot free? Yes. The free plan runs the daily reward features for up to 4 hours of bot time per game day, with no expiry.

Does it modify the game? No. It only reads the screen and taps through ADB, like a player.

Can my account be banned? Automation breaks most games' terms, and no bot can promise zero risk. Phantom plays at human speed with random pauses.
